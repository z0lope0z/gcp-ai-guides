Professional Machine Learning Engineer (PMLE)

Follows the exam guide dated June 1, 2026. Exam sections: low-code AI ~13%, data and models across teams ~16%, scaling prototypes ~21%, serving and scaling ~20%, pipelines ~18%, monitoring ~13%. Vertex AI is now Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform; the guides use the new names and map them to the old ones. Stars show how much exam weight a guide carries (★★★ heaviest).

Suggested study path

  1. Exam overview to see the scope and the renames.
  2. The two ML fundamentals guides: most scenario questions hinge on leakage, imbalance, overfitting and choosing the right metric.
  3. The three gen AI guides: Model Garden, Agent Platform, RAG failure modes and LLM evaluation.
  4. The six exam-section guides, in order, for the Google Cloud product choices.
  5. Retake each guide's quiz until you score above 85%, then review its gotchas list the day before the exam.
